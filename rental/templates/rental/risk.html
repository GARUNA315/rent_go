Risk Management & Terms

How Rent&Go keeps every trip safe — and exactly when, how and why you can claim.

What you can claim

During the rental period the following incidents can be claimed through Rent&Go:

  • Accidents — collision damage while riding/driving.
  • Damages — scratches, dents or interior damage caused during the trip.
  • Theft — vehicle stolen while in your custody.
  • Breakdowns — mechanical failure, towing & recovery.

Claims not covered: drunk driving, racing, off-roading, use as a taxi, unauthorised drivers, and damage hidden from the handover checklist.

FIR verification — how we check the claim is TRUE

Every accident, damage-from-collision or theft claim is verified before a single rupee is paid out:

  1. You file the claim with incident details, damage photos and date/time.
  2. FIR is mandatory. Register an FIR at the nearest police station and upload the FIR number, police-station name and a copy of the FIR document.
  3. Our risk team cross-checks the FIR document, the damage photos and the live GPS route & speed log recorded during the trip.
  4. The team marks the claim Verified genuine ✓ or Rejected ✕ with a public decision note.
  5. Genuine claims are approved for payout and then settled directly to your account.

Claims with a valid FIR and matching GPS evidence are almost always approved. Claims without an FIR are treated as unverified.

Insurance & RC

Every listed vehicle must carry a valid Registration Certificate (RC) and an active insurance policy. You can see these details on every vehicle page:

  • RC / registration number + RC photo.
  • Insurance policy number, provider and validity date.
  • Live badge showing insurance active / expiring / expired.

Only vehicles with unexpired insurance are actively promoted. Owners with an Verified identity are shown first.

GPS tracking as evidence

During every rental trip, the vehicle's position, route and speed are recorded (from the renter's GPS or an in-vehicle tracker). This log is used to:

  • Show the owner and renter where the vehicle is, live.
  • Prove whether the incident happened on the reported route.
  • Cross-check speeds against the reported cause of the accident.

Terms & Conditions

  • Identity: renters and owners must complete photo + driving licence + Aadhaar verification.
  • Licence: only validly licensed riders/drivers may operate a vehicle.
  • Payment: rental fees are collected before pickup; refunds follow the claims process.
  • Return: the vehicle must be returned on time. Late returns are billed at the daily rate.
  • Fuel & tolls: paid by the renter unless agreed otherwise.
  • Liability: Rent&Go is a marketplace. Physical vehicle liability during a trip sits with the renter, while financial claims (damage up to the insured value) are settled through this claims process.
  • Prohibited: smoking, pets, illegal activity, sub-leasing, racing, or carrying more passengers than the seat capacity.
  • Disputes: disagreements about a claim are settled using the FIR + GPS evidence above. False claims are reported to the police.

By booking on Rent&Go you accept these terms. Keep your receipts, photos and FIR copies — they protect you.
